# AgentPhone · 60s voiceover script

Voice: ElevenLabs **Becca** (warm, confident Australian), `eleven_multilingual_v2`, one take split
into lines. Music: ElevenLabs Music v2.5, 60s instrumental, 124 BPM. SFX: ElevenLabs text-to-sound
(ring, tap, whoosh, chime, typing). Files live in `public/audio/`; placement is in
`src/agentphone/Soundtrack60.tsx`.

| Time | Section | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cold open | An agent makes forty calls a day. How much of it reaches the CRM? |
| 0:05 | Logo | Meet AgentPhone. |
| 0:07 | Incoming call (rings → Accept) | Know who's calling, and why, before you answer. |
| 0:11 | Live transcript | Every call is transcribed live, picking out the details that matter as you talk. |
| 0:19 | After the call (summary writes itself) | Hang up, and the summary's already written. Tasks captured, and logged straight to your CRM. |
| 0:27 | Call score | Every call is scored, so you know exactly who to coach. |
| 0:32 | Desktop calls view | And the full picture is waiting on your desktop. |
| 0:38 | Desktop or mobile | Desktop or mobile, it's all in one place. |
| 0:43 | Messages | Texts sit in the same thread, with replies drafted for you. |
| 0:49 | Set up in minutes | And it plugs into the CRM you already use, in minutes. |
| 0:55 | Close | AgentPhone. Every call, in your CRM. Join the waitlist today. |

Sound design: two rings while the call comes in, a tap on Accept and on End call, a soft whoosh on
every screen slide, a chime on "Auto-logged to CRM" and when the drafted text is sent, and quiet
typing under the live transcript and the summary. The music ducks under each line; the mix is
normalised to −14 LUFS.

Notes: "forty calls a day" is a benchmark, not a measured figure. Swap it if you have Meson's own
number. "In minutes" stays deliberately vague; only say "two minutes" if that's true at launch.
